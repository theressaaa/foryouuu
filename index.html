happy birthday,
my love ♡

i hope this new age brings good things your way, one by one. i hope you always have reasons to smile, the courage to go after what you want, and people around you who make you feel loved.

i hope this year is kinder to you. more happy days, less things that make you tired, and more moments you'll look back on with a smile.

and if someday things get a little too much, please remember that you don't have to go through it alone.

you have me. you can always tell me about your day, the little things that happened, the random things on your mind, or anything that's been bothering you.

i might not always know what to say, and i might not always know how to make things better, but i'll always be here to listen.

so whenever you need someone to talk to, someone to stay, or just a place to rest for a while, you can always come to me.

i hope you know that you'll always have a home in me. ♡
